# Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | A person who orders food through FoodFlow; identified by a unique e-mail address. |
| **Restaurant** | A business that offers a menu, has a cuisine, a district, a delivery fee and (from I2) opening hours and a minimum basket. |
| **Menu item** | Something a restaurant sells, with a name, a category and a price. |
| **Cart** | The customer's current selection of menu items with quantities, from a single restaurant. |
| **Order** | A confirmed request to deliver the contents of a cart to an address; has a number, a status and a total. |
| **Order status** | Where an order is in its life cycle: placed, accepted, preparing, ready, picked up, delivered, rejected or cancelled. |
| **Courier** | A person who delivers orders; has a vehicle, a location and an availability. |
| **Delivery fee** | The amount charged for delivery; flat per restaurant in I1. |
| **Payment** | The settlement of an order: cash in I1. |
| **Promotion** | A rule that reduces the price of an order or of items. |
